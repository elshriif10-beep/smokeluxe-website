CUSTOMER REVIEWS - HOW TO MODERATE

1. In Netlify, enable Forms/Form detection if prompted, then redeploy this ZIP.
2. New customer reviews arrive in Netlify > Forms > customer-reviews (may be under Forms or Data).
3. No reviews are automatically published.
4. To publish an approved review, edit approved-reviews.json in the site ZIP, e.g.:
[
  {"name":"Customer first name","rating":5,"review":"Their genuine review text","approved":true}
]
5. Re-upload the updated site ZIP to Netlify. To remove a review, delete its entry and redeploy.
6. Keep reviews authentic, do not edit their sentiment; get permission to show names.

IMPORTANT: Netlify Forms submissions are NOT an automatic publishing/admin approval panel. Approval requires editing approved-reviews.json and redeploying. The public page shows only approved=true entries. Check Netlify Forms availability and any plan limits.
